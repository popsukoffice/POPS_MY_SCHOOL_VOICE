Before you start: your permission

Please read this carefully. You can ask a trusted adult to read it with you.

What is this questionnaire about?It asks about your experience of school: what makes it hard to go, what makes you worried or anxious, what happens when you cannot attend, and what might help. It is not a test and there are no right or wrong answers.
How will your answers be used?Your answers go to the Educational Psychologist at Positive Outcomes Psychology Service. They will use them to understand things from your point of view and to work out how best to help you.
Who might see your answers?To help you, the Educational Psychologist may share what you have said with your parents/carers and with adults in education (for example teachers, your SENCO or pastoral staff) who might be able to help.
Keeping you safeIf something you tell us suggests you, or someone else, may not be safe, the Educational Psychologist will need to tell the right people so that you can be protected.
You can skip any question you do not want to answer, and you can stop at any time.

This will be added to your answers so the Educational Psychologist knows who they are from.

Understanding Why School Feels Difficult

This questionnaire is about your experience of school. There are no right or wrong answers.

This is not a test.
It is not about blaming you, your family, teachers or school. It is about understanding what is going well for you and what you are good at, as well as what makes school difficult, what makes anxiety grow, what happens when you cannot attend, and what could help.

You can choose more than one answer. You do not have to write much. If you do not want to answer a question, you can choose “I would rather not say” where available.

If you tell us that you are unsafe or frightened, an adult supporting you may need to know so that they can help keep you safe.
